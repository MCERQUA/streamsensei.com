## Real-business-name collision check
Searched "Stream Sensei" across web, YouTube, Facebook, USPTO/Justia trademark databases.
- Found: a small YouTube channel "The Stream Sensei" (streamer tips/tricks content) and a
  low-activity Facebook page "StreamSensei". No subscriber-count/authority signal of a major
  brand. No registered USPTO trademark for "Stream Sensei" (checked Trademarkia/Justia/USPTO
  search results — only unrelated "SENSEI" marks in cosmetics, medical devices, dental software,
  gaming-training categories; "sensei" itself is used generically across many unrelated brands).
- Conclusion: real but minor naming overlap, not a legal/trademark blocker. Flagged in the
  final build report for Josh's awareness; site is positioned as a coaching AGENCY (not a
  YouTube personality), which is a different market position than the existing channel.

## Real-angle determination
"Stream" + "Sensei" does not fit any construction-trade or insurance framing. Validated market
research (WebSearch) confirms an active market for streamer/content-creator coaching services:
StreamScheme (streamscheme.com/services), Fiverr Twitch-channel gigs, YouTube growth-coaching
offers ($197-$497 per engagement range found in market scan). Built as a non-CCA-branded,
non-insurance lead-gen site: **StreamSensei — livestream & content-creator growth coaching**
for Twitch/YouTube/Kick creators (channel audits, 1-on-1 coaching, branding/overlay consulting,
content strategy, monetization strategy, stream setup/tech consulting).

Not CCA-affiliated — no CCA phone/address/branding used (canonical CCA contact info is
insurance-specific per the build brief and doesn't apply here). Contact channel is the
Netlify-form lead capture + a domain-based email (hello@streamsensei.com); no physical address
listed since the business is inherently remote/online coaching — listing a fabricated street
address would be dishonest.
